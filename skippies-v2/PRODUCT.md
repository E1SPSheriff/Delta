# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Singapore polytechnic and JC students aged 16–20 are the core audience, with the game also designed to work for ages 13–25. The primary setting is MamaShop at Singapore Polytechnic.

## Product Purpose

SKIPPIES makes BYO a lasting habit after Open House by turning real BYO and recycling actions into quick, satisfying creature-collection moments. Players should feel that their everyday choices make their creatures stronger and that their collection visibly proves their own consistency.

## Positioning

Every creature, star, evolution and stat increase comes from the player's real-world BYO and recycling actions. Status comes from consistency, not spending, luck, guilt or pay-to-win systems.

## Operating Context

Players use the game on a phone around campus, MamaShop, the canteen and daily travel. The core loop is: photo-check a real BYO or recycling action, hatch an egg, grow a collection, train and battle, then return to the habit. Seasons and a fair MamaShop reward make consistency visible without punishing missed days.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve all existing game rules, hatch odds, pity guarantee, progression, campaign, asynchronous arena, season reward, privacy and safety behavior.
- Keep the live-camera scan, duplicate fingerprint check, cooldown, daily limit, published odds and server battle verification accurate.
- Keep all tested element IDs and `data-*` attributes stable.
- No emoji in the UI, no external fonts or CDNs, WCAG AA contrast, 44px touch targets, reduced-motion support and safe-area handling.
- Under-13 users remain offline-only; names remain generated; there is no chat; location remains opt-in and approximate.
- This is a working prototype and must not claim that a photo proves a real-world action.

## Brand Commitments

The setting is MamaShop at Singapore Polytechnic. The voice should feel encouraging, quick and socially legible rather than preachy, guilt-driven or anxious. The product promise is: “My everyday choices make my creatures stronger, and it shows.”

## Evidence on Hand

The existing tested prototype and its README describe the complete game loop, rules, safety model and implementation constraints in `README.md`. Creature art, icons and all client behavior already exist in `public/`.

## Product Principles

1. Make the real-world action feel like a small win immediately.
2. Make effort and consistency visible in the collection and stats.
3. Keep rivalry friendly and rewards fair.
4. Nudge without guilt, streak anxiety or FOMO.
5. Preserve honest safety, privacy and rules communication.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain WCAG AA contrast, large touch targets, keyboard focus, reduced-motion behavior, safe-area insets and explicit disabled-state explanations. Copy must remain clear for players aged 13 and above.
