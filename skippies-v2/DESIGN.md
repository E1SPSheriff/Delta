# Design direction

<!-- impeccable:design-schema 1 -->

## Contract

**THESIS:** SKIPPIES is a campus pocket arcade built from the visual language of a Singapore MamaShop: the first viewport proves that a real BYO action becomes creature power immediately, refusing the generic wellness dashboard and generic monster-game card grid.

**OWN-WORLD:** Ink-navy night-market surfaces, warm paper slips, signal tangerine, lime and pool-blue accents, chunky printed outlines, receipt tabs, sticker-like creature stages and compact scoreboard typography. The system feels assembled from a campus counter, a stamped loyalty card and a pocket battle machine.

**STORY:** “I did a real thing, my buddy got stronger, and my friends can see it.” Players scan or recycle, hatch, build their team, then use the collection and battle surfaces to show progress without shame.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A branded app bar opens into a split “today’s run” panel: the buddy and its earned power sit beside the two real-world action buttons. The season card reads as a stamped campus pass, while the bottom navigation behaves like a machine selector.

**FORM:** Operate mode, replacement world. A stacked field-guide dashboard with a high-contrast counter top, a single dominant action zone and quieter evidence rows below. The visual world carries across home, Dex, battles, ranks, radar and flows.

## Palette and material

- Ink navy is the primary reading and battle surface; paper white is the main operating surface.
- Signal tangerine is the action color; lime marks earned progress; pool blue marks discovery and location.
- Corners are 14–20px on large surfaces, but controls remain compact and tactile.
- Shadows are offset, soft and directional, like stickers and counter signage.
- Patterns are reserved for the campus-pass and receipt motifs; no decorative gradients or generic glass.

## Typography

Use the system UI stack only. Headlines are dense, bold and slightly condensed by weight rather than by a novelty font. Uppercase labels are reserved for machine-like metadata and section markers, not every heading.

## Motion

Use a short press-and-lift response for controls, a single staggered arrival for the home run panel, and keep hatch/battle motion purposeful. Respect reduced motion.

## Responsive behavior

The mobile layout remains the primary experience. At wider widths, the app gains breathing room and a subtle counter edge without becoming a desktop dashboard. Touch targets stay at least 44px.
